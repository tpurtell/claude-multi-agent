# Worker contract

Complete only your assigned bounded task. Read the project's CLAUDE.md/AGENTS.md
and the relevant plan before editing. Follow the brief's authority and gates.

- Use the assigned worktree and branch; do not edit another worker's checkout.
  Preserve pre-existing user changes. Never start duplicate work on a live task.
- Verify actual tool, model and permission capabilities. Do not infer privilege
  or deployment authority from an unsandboxed launcher or a skill.
- Shared hardware, ports and caches need explicit ownership, a consistent lock
  order, timeouts and teardown of your own jobs. Do not kill others' jobs.
- Keep STATUS.md current: branch/head, worktree, changes, gates, owned jobs and
  remaining work. Before a reset/stop, checkpoint safely within your authority.
- Do not silently switch accounts or paid providers. Capacity is not quota;
  invalid input is not either. Report the first meaningful failure.
- Defaults, merges, tags and releases belong to the coordinator unless the brief
  explicitly delegates them. Report recommendations and the evidence for them.
- Finish with changes, branch/commits, test results, measured conditions, open
  issues and any jobs/resources still owned. Never include keys or auth tokens.
